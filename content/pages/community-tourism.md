## Immerse yourself in the local culture

Discover our community and everyday life in rural Rwanda. Visit our school, join a traditional dance or cooking class, and see how banana beer is made.

## Join a guided bike or hiking tour

Explore our beautiful region on a short ride or an adventure of several days. We provide everything you need, from mountain bikes to camping gear, and guide you to the prettiest places around.

## Visit our school and gift shop

You are welcome any day! Meet the children and teachers and learn about our projects. Our gift shop sells lovely handmade crafts, and every franc of profit goes to the school.

## Rent bikes or camping gear

Missing some camping gear for Akagera Park, or want to explore on your own? We rent out bikes and all the camping gear you need.
