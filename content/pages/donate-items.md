**Things we can always use:** school materials, clothing, hygiene products, toys, batteries and more.

## Bring them in person

Living in Rwanda, or on your way to Akagera National Park? Stop by. We are on Akagera Road, 7 km from the park entrance. [Open the map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/wWhxXjkbhT6WmpMGA).

## Send them to us

Can't visit? No problem. These ways work best, but we are open to new ideas:

- Buy items online at [ubuy.rw](https://www.ubuy.rw/) and have them delivered to us
- Send a parcel with DHL

In Rwanda, post codes and house numbers are not common, so don't worry if the address looks unusual:

> Akagera Road, KM 21
> Kayonza, Eastern Province
