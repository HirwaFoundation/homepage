## How to send money

Sending money to Rwanda is harder than to most countries. IBAN does not work there, and PayPal and Wise can send but not receive. These two ways work for us:

1. **NALA (recommended).** Install the NALA app, choose Rwanda and send the money to the mobile wallet of our founder Jean Claude Habihirwe, **+250 782 575 855**. We then move it to the foundation's official bank account. Our sponsorship site has a [step-by-step guide with screenshots](https://sponsor-hirwa-dream.super.site/sponsorship-payments-nala).
2. **PayPal via a volunteer.** If NALA does not work for you, [write to us](/contact/) and we'll send you the PayPal address of one of our volunteers.

Please let us know when you have sent money, so we can thank you and tell you what it paid for.

Donations are **not tax-deductible** in the EU, UK or USA, because we are registered in Rwanda. If you have an idea how to make giving easier, please tell us!
