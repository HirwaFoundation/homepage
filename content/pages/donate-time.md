As a volunteer you help our teachers, enrich the children's school day and support our mission. And you get to know the Rwandan way of life: cook with us, or come along to the local markets.

However you can help, you will be more than welcome. Thank you for considering it!

## How to apply

Tell us a little about yourself. We are happy to have you! [Get in touch](/contact/).

You can also find us on [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/) and [Worldpackers](https://www.worldpackers.com/).
