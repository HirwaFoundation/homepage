## Kinyarwanda classes

Many adults in our community cannot read or write Kinyarwanda. That makes everyday life hard, from reading basic information to communicating with others. Our classes give them a second chance to learn these skills and take part fully in society.

**Education is a right, no matter the age.**

## English classes

Tourism in Rwanda is growing fast, and English has become the bridge between visitors and locals. Our village is only 7 km from Akagera National Park, so it feels this boom directly. Speaking English lets people take part in it and benefit from it.

That is why we offer free English classes for children and adults. Breaking the language barrier enriches our community and invites visitors to discover our culture, our everyday life and our beautiful region.
