Many families in our village cannot afford health insurance. One illness can then mean debt, or no treatment at all.

We raise money to pay the yearly health insurance for these families. **Insurance costs about 2.5 USD per person per year.** It keeps people healthy, takes away a heavy financial burden and improves their quality of life.

**78 people now have health insurance thanks to your donations.** A big thank you goes to IMIGONGO Partners Ltd.
