In Rwanda, menstruation has long been a taboo, surrounded by stigma and misconceptions. For girls from poor families it is one more obstacle to school and to their wellbeing. Pads cost money, and rural schools often lack proper sanitation.

We give girls in need **free reusable hygiene products**, and we talk openly with girls and boys about reproductive health.

> **18 %** of women and girls in Rwanda miss school or work during their period because they can't afford menstrual products.

No girl should miss school because of a lack of menstrual products or information.
