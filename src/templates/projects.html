Welcome to Kariyeri

Our projects

Life in our village revolves around farming and community. There is no electricity and no running water. In the dry season children carry water home, often several kilometres, several times a day. Many families don't have enough food or any health care. Our projects tackle these roots of hardship, with the children and their education at the centre.
